The full Check pipeline — retrieve, select sentences, judge, decide — on held-out claims, run offline with a hosted judge. Each verdict sits beside the answer-key label, and the sentences are quoted from the papers, not written by the model.

● matches the key · ○ disagrees · colour is the verdict: supported · contradicted · unsupported
What this tab does and does not do. It retrieves — it does not judge. Everything runs in your browser: the demo corpus is precomputed, a live corpus is fetched from PubMed and embedded on your machine, and your claim is embedded locally with the same 23 MB model. No key and no backend; the only requests are to PubMed and, once, for the model.

Press Negate the claim and watch the same papers come back. That is not a bug — a claim and its negation embed at cosine ≈ 0.93, so retrieval is a recall device and cannot tell them apart. Deciding support versus contradiction is a separate reading step; the Judged examples tab shows it.
